## Code of Conduct

Our team is committed to working in a respectful, professional, ethical, and collaborative manner. All members are responsible for contributing positively to the team environment and maintaining high standards of professional conduct, software quality, security, and academic integrity throughout the development of the project.

## 1. Respect, Equality and Inclusion

All team members will treat one another with respect, fairness, and professionalism. Discrimination, bullying, harassment, offensive language, personal attacks, and disrespectful behaviour will not be tolerated.

Different opinions, perspectives, and constructive disagreements will be welcomed and considered respectfully.

## 2. Professional Communication

Team members will communicate clearly, honestly, and respectfully through agreed communication channels.

Important project information, decisions, problems, risks, and changes will be shared with relevant team members in a timely manner.

Members will listen to alternative viewpoints and communicate constructively to avoid unnecessary conflict or misunderstanding.

## 3. Responsibility and Accountability

Each member is responsible for completing their agreed tasks to an appropriate standard and within the agreed timeframe.

If a member encounters a problem or expects a delay, they will inform the team as early as possible so that the issue can be addressed collectively.

Members will take responsibility for their own work, decisions, and actions.

## 4. Scrum Participation

All members will actively participate in agreed Scrum activities, including:

- Sprint Planning
- Daily Stand-ups
- Sprint Reviews
- Sprint Retrospectives

Team members will contribute to planning, estimation, problem-solving, progress reporting, and continuous improvement.

Members will respect agreed Sprint commitments and the team's Definition of Done.

## 5. Collaboration and Support

The team will work collaboratively rather than focusing only on individual achievements.

Members will:

- Share relevant knowledge.
- Support others when difficulties arise.
- Contribute to solving problems collectively.
- Treat genuine mistakes as opportunities for learning and improvement rather than as reasons for blame.

## 6. Constructive Feedback and Code Review

Team members will give and receive constructive feedback professionally and respectfully.

Code reviews will focus on improving:

- Code quality
- Security
- Maintainability
- Reliability

Code reviews will focus on the work rather than criticising individuals.

Members are expected to consider reasonable feedback and make appropriate improvements.

## 7. GitHub and Version Control

All project work will be managed through the agreed GitHub workflow.

Members will:

- Use appropriate branches.
- Write meaningful commit messages.
- Use pull requests where required.
- Follow the team's agreed review and merging process.
- Respect protected and shared branches.
- Avoid actions that could compromise project history or shared work.

Changes to protected or shared branches must follow the team's agreed review and merging process.

## 8. Code Quality and Testing

Team members will maintain professional standards of software quality throughout development.

Code should be:

- Understandable
- Maintainable
- Appropriately documented
- Consistent with agreed project practices

New functionality should be tested appropriately.

Known defects or failed tests must be reported and addressed rather than deliberately ignored or concealed.

## 9. Security, Privacy and Authorisation

Team members will protect project data, credentials, personal information, and other confidential information.

Systems, repositories, databases, and development resources will only be accessed or modified with appropriate authorisation.

Passwords, API keys, access tokens, and other sensitive credentials must not be publicly exposed or unnecessarily shared.

## 10. Academic Integrity

All submitted work must represent the team's genuine contribution.

The following are prohibited:

- Plagiarism
- Fabrication of results
- Falsification of evidence
- Unauthorised copying
- Claiming another person's work as one's own

Contributions, individual responsibilities, and assessment evidence will be represented honestly and accurately.

## 11. Transparency and Evidence

The team will maintain transparent and accurate records of:

- Project progress
- Decisions
- Issues
- Contributions
- Testing
- Releases

Evidence used for assessment must accurately reflect the work completed by the team.

No member will deliberately hide problems, failed tests, defects, risks, or incomplete work.

## 12. Ethical Decision-Making

When making technical and project decisions, the team will consider their potential impact on:

- Users
- Society
- Privacy
- Security
- Other stakeholders

The team will aim to avoid harm and act honestly, fairly, responsibly, and in accordance with professional computing ethics.

## 13. Conflict Resolution

Disagreements will be addressed respectfully and as early as possible.

Team members will first attempt to resolve conflicts through open discussion and constructive problem-solving.

If an issue cannot be resolved within the team, it will be raised with the **Scrum Master**.

If the matter remains unresolved or requires additional guidance, the team will seek appropriate assistance from the **Module Teaching Team (Lecturer/Tutor)**.

## 14. Continuous Improvement

The team will use Sprint Retrospectives to identify:

- What worked well
- What did not work well
- What should be improved

The team will agree on practical improvement actions and apply them in subsequent Sprints.

Lessons learned will be used to improve:

- Teamwork
- Communication
- Development practices
- Software quality

## 15. Commitment to the Team

Every member is expected to contribute actively and support the team's shared objectives.

The team will prioritise the successful delivery of a high-quality product over individual interests and will maintain a professional, respectful, ethical, and supportive working environment throughout the project.

## Team Agreement

By working on this project, all team members agree to follow this **Code of Conduct** and uphold the principles of:

**Respect, Equality, Honesty, Responsibility, Accountability, Collaboration, Professionalism, Security, Privacy, Academic Integrity, Ethical Decision-Making, and Continuous Improvement.**
